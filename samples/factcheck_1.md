## CRITICAL ISSUES FOUND

### ISSUE #1: Mathematical Error in Revenue Growth Claim — CRITICAL
**Location:** Executive Summary, Line 8  
**Claim:** "Revenue has grown 4.8x since FY24 to ₹22,623.58 crore in FY26"  
**Sources Cited:** [4], [5], [6]

**Fact-Check Result:** INCORRECT  
- **FY24 Revenue (from zepto.md line 70):** ₹4,454.52 crore  
- **FY26 Revenue (from zepto.md line 72):** ₹22,623.58 crore  
- **Actual Calculation:** 22,623.58 ÷ 4,454.52 = **5.08x**, NOT 4.8x  
- **Verification:** Both zepto.md and the report cite the exact same revenue figures; the multiplier is mathematically wrong

**Impact:** This is a factual error that overstates the claimed growth rate by ~0.2x. While the underlying data is sourced correctly, the interpretation is incorrect.

**Fix Required:** Change "4.8x" to "5.1x" or "approximately 5x" OR recalculate from correct FY-to-FY period if that was the intended comparison.
